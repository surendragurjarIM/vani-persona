**Baseline:** 11.5% of answered VANI calls fix a meeting, with one script for everyone.

**What the evidence says a persona can recover** (observational; 95% CI, stratified by lead type):
- Repeat-called sellers: 1st call 16.3% vs 4th+ call 4.3%. Their persona gets a short, acknowledging opening and immediate slots.
- Pacing: slower pitch 33.3% vs 23.1%.
- Interest: offering a slot right after interest 61.0% vs 27.2%; after rush, 30.1% vs 12.1%. These drive the live switch.
- The existing personalised-script trial converted 14.2% vs 11.6% for the main bot. This is weak evidence, shown for direction only.

**Switch quality:** the live tool, replayed on 290 audited calls, is in the right mode on the labelled turn for frustration 72%, rush 65%, confusion 34% and interest 30%, with zero flip-flopping (a 2-turn cooldown). Detector F1 (LLM + lexicon): frustration 0.77, rush 0.67.

**Distinctness:** 9,002 persona configurations across 1,49,363 sellers; the largest covers only 1.5% of sellers.

**Live demo:** all 3 test calls on Sarvam fixed a meeting after mid-call switches, and each closing repeated the agreed day, time and place.

**Next step:** an A/B pilot campaign per agent, measuring meeting-fix rate and drop-offs against today's VANI.
