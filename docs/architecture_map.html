One call in motion · Warm Hinglish, seller in Noida, 6th attempt

Steps 6 to 9 inside a single call

Turn 1"haan ji, boliye"Step 6 · persona openingShort, acknowledges the earlier calls, offers two slots for tomorrow.
Turn 2"kya bola? samjha nahi"Step 7 · confusion (LLM + keywords)Apologises, explains in simpler words, checks understanding.
Turn 3"busy hoon, jaldi bolo"Step 7 · rush (LLM + keywords)One line: tomorrow 10 am or 6 pm?
Turn 4"shaam chhe baje, dukaan pe"Seller gave a timeAccepts at once and confirms day, time and place.
Close"haan sahi hai"Step 9 · meeting fixed, log savedRepeats the slot, says the executive calls first, thanks once.
Deployment

Nothing needs to run on a laptop

Sarvam cloud

Live now
  • Six VANI agents, committed and versioned
  • Python switch tool in Sarvam's sandbox
  • Regional voices, call logs, recordings
  • Board: VANI · Persona Performance
  • Nothing for IndiaMART to host

IndiaMART cloud

To deploy
  • Persona Service container (Docker image is ready)
  • Scheduler for the nightly and weekly jobs
  • Postgres for results, object storage for data and personas
  • Sarvam keys in a secret manager
  • HTTPS address for webhooks and the on-start hook; allow Sarvam's IP 4.213.167.70
  • Console behind company login

Suggested: AWS Mumbai (App Runner or ECS) or GCP Mumbai (Cloud Run), whichever IndiaMART already uses.
